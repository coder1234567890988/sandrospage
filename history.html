Humanities Symposium:

At school, I participated in the humanities symposium which wanted students to create and present a slideshow on a certain topic in humanities whether that be History, or Physcology etc... That would then be presented online. For this I chose history, and I made a presentation on the rapid technological advancements made during the Sengoku Period. For resources I first watched a general summary of the Sengoku Period on youtube. The video helped me understand general changes in Japan throughout the Sengoku Period whether that be through changes in economy, or infrastucture or also cultural change. And it was around an hour long. Afterwards, I used sources like the japanese wikipedia(this is just the version of wikipedia they have in Japan, which had translations in english) along with various websites dedicated to teaching about the Sengoku period to build more substantial knowledge in the topics that I wanted to talk about. These topics included rapid infrastructal improvements including castle technology and advancements in military tactics along with improvements in armaments and naval technology. Although this is not directly related to CS, I do believe it has improved my research skills and critical thinking which are neccesary in CS. Additionally, having to present and break down the core topics to the viewer has helped improve my public speaking and general confidence, which is important when you are working on a software with a team.
